From the dunes

Before you
hit the dunes.

Anything we haven't covered, ask us on WhatsApp — we usually reply in minutes.

What time is pickup?+

Between 14:30 and 15:30 depending on your area. We confirm the exact time the day before.

Is hotel pickup included?+

Yes — return transfers from any Dubai hotel or residence are included in every package.

Where do you pick up from?+

All of Dubai. Sharjah and Abu Dhabi pickups are possible with a supplement — ask us.

Is BBQ dinner included?+

Yes. Charcoal BBQ buffet with vegetarian options, salads, and unlimited soft drinks at the camp.

Are quad bikes included?+

Only in the Quad Bike and Quad + Premium packages, or as a paid add-on to any safari.

Is it suitable for children?+

Yes. Tell us their ages and we'll arrange a gentler drive and a child seat if needed.

Can pregnant guests join?+

Dune bashing isn't recommended. We can arrange a direct transfer to the camp instead.

What should I wear?+

Light, comfortable clothing and closed shoes. Evenings in the desert get cooler — bring a layer.

Is this shared or private?+

Standard, Premium and Quad packages use a shared 4×4 (max 6 guests). Private 4×4 is priced per vehicle.

What is the cancellation policy?+

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup. Same-day cancellations are non-refundable.

How can I pay?+

Cash to the driver, card, or bank transfer. Payment link on request when you book on WhatsApp.
